# Community conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Discuss code and ideas without personal attacks. Help people participate regardless of experience, disability, nationality, race, religion, gender, or sexual orientation.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, and sharing someone's private information are not acceptable. Respect requests to stop an interaction.

Maintainers may remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation to protect the community. They should explain decisions privately when appropriate and avoid exposing the reporter's identity.

Report concerns to Matthew through the contact form at [crossinginto.ai](https://crossinginto.ai). Include only the information needed to understand the incident. Do not post sensitive reports in a public issue.
