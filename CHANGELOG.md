# Changelog

## Unreleased

- Crossing Into family styling (Luminous Commons, Dusk): Fraunces, Hanken Grotesk, and JetBrains Mono bundled for offline use; lifted panes, glass dialogs, and sun amber as the brand signature across themes.
- Animated Bloch-sphere mark (superposition → measurement → eigenstate) in the header, colophon, and a labelled version in "Why Eigenstate?"; SVG geometry is tested and holds still when paused or quiet.

## 0.1.0 — 2026-09-19

- Persistent seeded simulation with bounded agents, experiments, branches, quantum telemetry, inference activity, and latent world state.
- Deterministic bounded catch-up after closed or hidden sessions.
- Versioned IndexedDB snapshots, checksums, migration, backup recovery, and multi-tab writer ownership.
- Seven coordinated panes, two asymmetric layouts, theme selection, fullscreen, quiet mode, and diagnostic controls.
- Eigenstate, Tokyo Night, Synthwave '84, Nord, and Catppuccin Mocha palettes.
- Stillpoint-style theme cycling and colophon, plus optional locally synthesized hum.
- Portable universe export/import, confirmed reset, and cached offline shell.
- Occasional simulated universe crashes with a fault screen, restart countdown, configurable frequency, and an exportable archive.
- Cloudflare static hosting configuration and Crossing Into tool listing.
